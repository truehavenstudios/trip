Privacy

Trip Planner by TrueHaven Studios – last updated October 2026.

The planner runs in your browser and saves your trips on your device. There is no account and no email address.

Trip groups

A trip group shares one copy of the trip between the people in it. Before anything is sent, it is locked (AES-256-GCM) on your device with a key that comes from the group code. The sync server, a Cloudflare Worker with a small database, only stores scrambled data it cannot read, a random id, a version number and timestamps. Anyone with the group code or invite link can open the trip, so share it only with your travel group – you can make a new code at any time in Group settings, which locks the old one out. "Delete for everyone" erases the server copy. We do not sell, share or use the data and cannot recover it for you.

Photos

Photos you add are made small on your device and travel the same locked way. They are never posted anywhere else.
